About the company

A Tema labour company built around dependable workforce provision

GET Labour Company Limited — known as GLCL — supplies labour to businesses around Tema. This page explains who we are, the mission and values recorded in our company documents, and how we operate.

Our story

Established in the Tema Port enclave

GLCL was set up to connect businesses that need reliable labour with people looking for honest work. Company records place the company’s founding in July 2013. The company has focused on the practical side of workforce supply — understanding what an operation needs, matching workers to those needs and keeping the arrangement organised on the ground.

An earlier company document, dated 4 April 2011, references a licence application to operate as a labour supply company at Tema, Takoradi and other industries in the West African sub-region. The company’s historical business plan describes the development of labour supply operations around these ports, and the wider industrial environment of Ghana.

The company is based on Tema Meridian Road, around Tema Community 2 — within easy reach of the port, terminals, warehouses and industrial facilities that make up its daily operating environment.

What GLCL does

GLCL supplies four categories of workforce: skilled labour, general (non-skilled) labour, equipment operators, and supervisors and operational support personnel. These cover onboard, ashore, transit and topside activities, as well as site, warehouse and industrial work. Recruitment and workforce preparation sit at the centre of the business: finding the right people and preparing them for the work.

Mission & vision

What the company was built to do

Taken from the company’s own business documents — the standard GLCL sets for its labour supply and service delivery.

Mission

To recruit and prepare skilled and unskilled workers for employment — particularly at the Tema and Takoradi ports and in the wider industrial environment — through reliable labour supply, training and retraining.

Vision

“Excellence in labour supply and service delivery.”

Core values

  • Endurance — The stamina to keep performing through the demands of real port and industrial work.
  • Reliability — Dependable labour supply — workers and service delivery that clients can count on.
  • Punctuality — Respect for schedules, reporting times and the operational requirements of every assignment.
  • Honesty — Straightforward dealing with clients and workers alike, with no room for surprises.
  • Customer friendliness — Professional, approachable service — from the first enquiry through to allocation on site.

How we work

Professional workforce provision, handled properly

Requirements first

Every placement starts with the client’s actual requirement — the role, the numbers, the location and the working arrangements — so nothing is left to assumption.

Worker vetting

Workers are assessed against the requirements of each assignment before allocation, so clients receive people suited to the work being asked of them.

Supervision on site

The company works with supervisors, foremen and support personnel who organise allocated labour, keep records and coordinate with client site teams.

Safe working practices

Attention to safe working practices is part of how GLCL operates, with a safety officer within the company structure alongside operations and allocation roles.

People and organisation

Structured for operational work

Labour supply only works when allocation, supervision and administration are handled properly. GLCL’s structure includes operations management, allocation roles, supervisory staff and administrative support — the functions needed to organise workers for port, maritime and industrial assignments.

For clients, this means a clear point of contact when workforce needs change. For workers, it means assignments are organised, accounted for and supported by people whose job is to keep operations running smoothly.

Workforce development

Training and retraining, before placement

The company’s business documents describe recruiting workers and preparing them for employment: training and retraining skilled and unskilled people so they can handle equipment and modern working methods on the job. This preparation is what turns a pool of workers into a workforce an operation can rely on.

Whether specific training arrangements are available now depends on current requirements — the office can confirm what the company offers today.

Worker welfare

Responsible labour supply, on both sides

Protecting workers against the injuries and health risks of employment is one of the documented objectives of the company. How that objective is met in practice on any assignment depends on the client’s site, the nature of the work and the arrangements agreed for that operation.

  • Assignments are organised with site safety requirements and supervision in mind
  • Workers are matched to the requirements of the operation before allocation
  • Client site rules, inductions and PPE requirements are part of the arrangement
  • Welfare-related questions can be raised with the GLCL office directly

Specific employment conditions, benefits and protective arrangements are confirmed per assignment between GLCL, the client and the worker.
