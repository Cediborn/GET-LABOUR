What happens next

  1. Your request reaches the GLCL operations team by email.
  2. The team reviews the requirement against current availability.
  3. GLCL contacts you to discuss details and confirm what is possible.
  4. Any arrangement is confirmed directly between GLCL and your company.

Each request is assessed individually. Submitting a request does not by itself confirm that workers are available.
