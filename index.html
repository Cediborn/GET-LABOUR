Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What types of labour does GLCL provide?

GLCL provides four main categories: skilled labour, general (non-skilled) labour, equipment operators, and supervisors and operational support personnel. General labour includes onboard, ashore, transit and topside gangs. Equipment operators include forklift, reach stacker and crane operators.

How do I request workers?

Fill in the labour request form on this website with your requirements, or call us on +233 30 396 1288 or +233 55 800 7876. You can also email getlabourcompanylimited@gmail.com. We will discuss availability and arrangements with you directly.

What information should I include in a labour request?

The type of work, how many workers you need, the location, when you need them and for how long. If specific equipment experience, certifications or site safety requirements are involved, include those as well. The more detail you give, the better the match can be.

How can I apply for work with GLCL?

Submit the employment application form on this website with your details, preferred work category and a short summary of your experience. You can also contact us by phone or email. Applying registers your interest — it does not guarantee placement.

Does submitting an application guarantee employment?

No. Placement depends on the company’s operational requirements at the time, client demand and whether your profile suits a specific assignment. Registering your interest keeps your details available for suitable opportunities.

Where is GLCL located?

GLCL is located on Tema Meridian Road, around Tema Community 2 in Tema, Ghana, within the Tema Port enclave.

How can I contact the company?

Call +233 30 396 1288 or +233 55 800 7876, or email getlabourcompanylimited@gmail.com. Contact details are also on the contact page with a map link to the Tema office area.
