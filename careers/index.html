For job seekers

Work with GLCL

GLCL supplies people to port, maritime, logistics and industrial operations around Tema. If you are looking for steady work — and you are ready when the call comes — register your interest with us.

How it works

Register once, stay in the pool

Work through GLCL comes from client requirements — when a business needs people for an operation, the team looks through registered candidates and contacts those who fit. That means your details need to be accurate and your phone reachable.

Registering does not guarantee employment. Placement depends on client demand, the type of work available at the time, and whether your profile suits a specific assignment. Be wary of anyone asking you to pay money for placement — GLCL does not charge workers to register.

Categories of work

What kind of work may be available

These are the categories GLCL supplies people for. Actual vacancies change with client requirements — there are no fixed positions advertised on this page.

General labour

Gang and individual work for onboard, ashore, transit and topside activities, plus warehouse, depot and site manpower. No advanced qualifications required — reliability and willingness to work matter most.

Skilled labour

Trade, driving and clerical roles such as drivers, tally clerks, record clerks and delivery clerks. Relevant experience or certification is valued for these roles.

Equipment operators

Forklift, reach stacker and crane operation. Experience with the specific equipment — and any certification you hold — will be part of the discussion.

Supervisory & support roles

Supervisors, foremen and support personnel who organise and account for allocated labour on site. Previous supervisory experience is typically required.

The process

From application to assignment

  1. Register your interest

    Submit the application form with your details, preferred category and a short summary of your experience.

  2. Details reviewed

    The GLCL team reviews your profile against the categories of work the company supplies people for.

  3. Assignment matching

    When a client requirement matches your profile, the team contacts you about the specific assignment.

  4. Placement arranged

    If both sides agree, the arrangement — location, dates and working conditions — is confirmed directly with you.
