Privacy policy

This policy explains what happens to information you submit through the GLCL website. It is a working draft written to match how this website actually operates.

i

Draft status. This policy describes the actual data handling of this website. Before publication it should be reviewed and approved by company management, and by a qualified legal professional where required.

1. Who we are

GET Labour Company Limited (“GLCL”, “we”) is a labour supply company based on Tema Meridian Road, around Tema Community 2, Tema, Ghana. Contact for privacy questions: getlabourcompanylimited@gmail.com or +233 55 800 7876.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to submit through this website:

  • Labour request forms — contact name, company or organisation, telephone number, email address, the labour category requested, work details you describe, preferred contact method, and any optional information you add.
  • Employment application forms — name, telephone number, email address (optional), preferred work category, skills and experience, certifications, general location, personal statement, and your consent record.
  • General enquiry forms — name, email address, telephone number (optional) and your message.

We do not ask for national identification documents, bank details, dates of birth or other sensitive personal information through this website, and we ask that you do not submit them.

3. Why we collect it

Submitted information is used for one purpose only: to respond to you and to assess and administer the request or application you made — for example, discussing a labour requirement with your company, or considering your suitability for work when a matching requirement arises.

4. How submissions are delivered

Forms on this website are delivered to GLCL by email using EmailJS (emailjs.com), a third-party form-delivery service. When you submit a form, your entries are sent to GLCL’s email inbox through that service. If delivery fails, the website offers you a pre-filled email so you can send the same information yourself — your submission is never silently discarded or falsely confirmed.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (pages.github.com). Standard server logs (including IP addresses) may be recorded by the host for security and operations purposes, under GitHub’s own privacy policy.

5. Who can see your information

Your submission is visible to GLCL staff who handle enquiries, labour requests and employment applications. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for marketing. We do not publish applicant details on this website.

6. How long we keep it

Labour requests are kept as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any resulting arrangement, and for a reasonable period afterwards for record-keeping. Application details are kept while they remain relevant to potential placements. If you want your details removed, email getlabourcompanylimited@gmail.com with your name and the phone number you used, and we will delete them from our records.

7. Security

Submissions travel over encrypted HTTPS connections and arrive in GLCL’s email inbox. Access is limited to staff who need it for their work. No internet system is completely secure, so please avoid submitting sensitive personal information through website forms in the first place.

8. Your rights

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by contacting the details above. We will respond to reasonable requests as quickly as practical.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website or its data handling changes. The latest version is always published on this page.

Last updated: October 2026 — drafted to match the live behaviour of this website (form delivery via EmailJS, hosting on GitHub Pages).
